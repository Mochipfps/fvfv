HOW IT WORKS

The XCOPUNKS staking and reward system, start to finish.

1

You stake XCOPUNKS NFTs — the NFT is the asset you stake.

2

Each staked XCOPUNKS NFT represents one equal reward share.

3

Each NFT is locked for 7 days from the moment it is staked.

4

90% of the total NFT Mint Fee is allocated to $XCPN rewards for users who stake XCOPUNKS NFTs.

5

10% of the NFT Mint Fee is allocated to build / project.

6

The separate 10% Creator Fee has a 7% allocation for ETH rewards to users who stake XCOPUNKS NFTs.

7

3% of the Creator Fee is allocated to build / project.

8

More actively staked XCOPUNKS NFTs means a larger share of the active reward pools.

9

Each NFT has its own 7-day unlock time, tracked individually.

10

Claim and unstake become available after each NFT’s own lock ends.

11

Season 1 starts on 14 August at 14:00 UTC.

12

Season 1 ends on 21 August at 14:00 UTC.
