Two separate clocks. The campaign entry window is open for 24 hours or until 2,000 NFTs have entered, whichever comes first. Each NFT then runs its own 24-hour reward period from the exact moment it was staked — an NFT staked at 10:30 UTC unlocks at 10:30 UTC the next day.
CLAIM & UNSTAKE RULE
  1. During its own 24 hours, the NFT stays locked: claim and unstake are both disabled.
  2. When its 24 hours end, the reward claim becomes available.
  3. Unstake unlocks only after that NFT's reward has been claimed and the transaction is confirmed.
  4. There is no further waiting after a confirmed claim — unstake immediately.
Each NFT is tracked separately against its original staker. NFTs staked at different times unlock at different times.
